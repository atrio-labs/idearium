This text is a draft. The vision document does not define conduct rules, so this is their first design, to be reviewed by the maintainers and then discussed in a public RFC. It is inspired by the practices of open communities, without copying text from anyone else.

## What we expect

- **Discuss the idea, not the person.** Disagree with arguments. Challenging an RFC is part of the process.
- **Respect the pseudonym.** Nobody has to show who they are. Do not try to find out or reveal another person's identity.
- **Give credit.** Whoever proposes, comments and builds appears in the credits. Do not erase anyone's contribution.
- **Do not measure merit by title or fame.** There is no hierarchy of merit here. A good question is worth more than a good résumé.
- **Leave without drama.** Anyone who enrolls can leave at any time, and leaving is public and normal.

## What we do not accept

- **Harassment**: stalking, personal attacks, repeated insults, intimidation.
- **Discrimination**: attacking or demeaning people for origin, race, gender, orientation, disability, religion, age or class.
- **Exposing third parties' personal data** (doxxing): address, phone, documents, photos or any data about someone without their consent.
- **Spam and commercial self-promotion**: ads, sales links, product promotion unrelated to the RFC.
- **Illegal content.**

## How to report

Moderation works through reports, with a report button on content. There is no approval queue before publishing: moderation happens afterwards, based on reports. Describe what happened and where. You do not need to prove anything beyond that.

## What happens next

The maintainers read the report, look at the context and respond. Depending on the case, they may ask for the content to be adjusted, hide it, or suspend the account. Suspension is reversible. The history of a suspended account stays, anonymized, so that discussions other people built on top of it do not break.

## How to appeal

Anyone who was suspended or had content removed can contest the decision. The appeal is read by the maintainers. The decision can be reviewed.
