This is the preliminary governance of the project. It is small on purpose, and it will grow through public RFCs.

## Co-creators and initial maintainers

Idearium has been maintained by two people from the start:

- **Kae Brazauskas**: backend, infrastructure, data modeling, the vision document and the backend roadmap.
- **Gabriele**: frontend, visual identity, home page design (the Matéria direction), editorial tone and microcopy.

We do not use the word *founder*, by choice: it sounds like a startup. We are **co-creators** and **initial maintainers** of a digital commons.

## How we decide

Decisions about product, identity and vision are made by consensus between the two people who maintain the project.

- **Backend and infrastructure:** Kae leads, and aligns with Gabriele on whatever affects the experience (shape of API responses, flows, performance).
- **Frontend, design and tone:** Gabriele leads, and aligns with Kae on whatever affects the contract with the API.
- **Moderation:** done by the maintainers, with an appeal process. Someone who was suspended can contest it. See the [code of conduct](/en/codigo-de-conduta).
- **New lab:** starts as a public RFC, discussed in the Meta Lab (to be created).
- **Changes to the vision document:** only through a public RFC. An important change does not happen by the decision of one person alone.

Maintainer is not a rank. It is a responsibility: moderating and answering for what the platform does.

## What does not change

Three points are immutable. Changing them would require refounding the project:

1. **License of public content: CC BY-SA 4.0.**
2. **No financial mediation.** The platform does not touch money between people.
3. **Respected pseudonym.** Nobody is required to show a real identity.

## How new maintainers join

After the first 100 active users, we will consider inviting one or two more people as maintainers, by explicit choice. The criteria are visible constructive contribution and alignment with the vision document.
