*Version of 10 October 2026.*

## Who can use it

Anyone can read without an account. To write, comment and enroll in ideas, you need an account. Idearium is free and stays free: see [transparency](/en/transparencia).

## Your account

- You pick a `@username`, which is the only identity shown. A pseudonym is allowed and is the default.
- Keep your password safe. Whoever signs in with it acts in your name.
- One person, one account. Accounts made to deceive, inflate votes or dodge a suspension may be removed.
- You can delete the account whenever you want, at `/excluir-conta`.

## What you publish

Everything you publish on Idearium (ideas, versions, comments, enrollments) is licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. In practice:

- anyone may copy, adapt and republish your text, including for commercial purposes, as long as they give credit and use the same license on derivatives;
- you must have the right to publish what you publish. Do not paste third-party text without a compatible license;
- the license cannot be undone: if you delete the account, people who already copied the text may keep using it, and the text published here stays in the history, anonymized as “[conta removida]”;
- no idea has an owner once published. The original author gets permanent credit, not control.

Do not write anything there that you do not want to be public and reused.

## Conduct and moderation

The rules for living together are in the [code of conduct](/en/codigo-de-conduta). Moderation happens after publication: there is no approval queue, and anyone can report. Maintainers answer reports, can hide content and suspend accounts, and a suspension can be contested.

## What is not allowed

Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, attempts to bypass security, automated collection that overloads the service, and publishing other people’s personal data.

## The service

Idearium is run by volunteers and is provided as is, with no guarantee of availability. It may go offline or change. We do not handle money between people and we are not a party to any agreement you make outside it.

## Privacy

What we do with your data is in the [privacy policy](/en/privacidade).

## Changes and contact

Changes to these terms go through public proposal, and the date at the top says which version is in force. Questions: [mantenedores@idearium.example](mailto:mantenedores@idearium.example). The jurisdiction and the details of the responsible entity will be published here before launch.
